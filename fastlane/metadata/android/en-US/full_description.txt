Alternate is a privacy-focused Android app that tells you who is calling without adding them to your phone's contact list. Perfect for numbers you only need to recognise for a while, because nothing you save here shows up in WhatsApp, Telegram or any other app.

<br><b>Features:</b>

- Caller ID popup over incoming and outgoing calls
- Contacts stay private: never written to your phone's contacts or synced anywhere
- Shows your saved names in the Google Phone app's call screen
- Several numbers per contact, with labels and country
- Groups, favorites and fast A-Z scrolling
- Search across names, numbers, notes and groups
- Import and export vCard (.vcf) files
- App lock with PIN and fingerprint
- Material 3 Expressive design with dynamic color and AMOLED theme
- Works fully offline: no internet permission

Keep your contact list clean while never missing an important call again!
