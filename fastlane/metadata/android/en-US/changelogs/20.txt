v3.3.0 - Backups, sorting and CSV
 - Encrypted backups to a folder you choose, daily or weekly, locked with a password or a recovery key
 - Restore contacts from a backup file
 - Sort contacts by name, date added, last modified, phone number or country
 - Contacts now remember when they were added and last edited
 - Export contacts as CSV to edit in a spreadsheet, then import them back
